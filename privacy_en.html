Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-12-19

This text explains what data is processed in the Color Chaos app.

1) Data types

  • Gameplay data: scores, selected mode, gameplay statistics.
  • Device/app data: anonymous identifiers that may be required for ads and performance measurement.
  • Sign-in data (optional): if you sign in with Game Center / Play Games, your username/ID is provided by the platform.

2) How we use data

  • Provide gameplay, show stats, manage credits.
  • (If enabled) show ads and enforce ad limits.
  • (If enabled) submit scores and show leaderboards.

3) Local storage

  • Some settings (e.g., sound, language), tutorial/onboarding state, and game credits may be stored locally on your device.

4) Third-party services

  • Services such as Google Mobile Ads and Game Center / Play Games may be used.
  • Their own privacy policies apply.

5) Sharing

  • We do not sell your data. Sharing may be limited to what’s required for third-party services to function.

6) Contact

  • For privacy requests, contact us via the developer contact on the store listing.
