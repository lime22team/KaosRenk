About

Last updated: 2025-12-19

Color Chaos is a fast-paced color reflex game.

How to play

  • Goal: Tap the correct option based on the color word shown.
  • Timer: Each question has a time limit; time-outs cost a life.
  • Lives: Wrong choices also cost a life.

Optional features

  • Sign in with Game Center / Play Games to sync and submit scores.
  • You may be able to watch ads to gain extra game credits.

Thanks for playing!
